Getting started (first time)

  1. Open your personal link (the admin gives it to you) and log in.
  2. Upload your CV. The agent reads it and fills in your details, education and experience.
  3. Check your details and tick the job types you want. These come from your CV, so everyone gets different jobs.
  4. Click Search jobs now. After that, the search runs by itself every day at 8:00 AM.

🏠 Dashboard

  • Search jobs now - find new jobs straight away (about 1 minute).
  • Pause / Resume daily search - stop or restart the 8:00 AM search.
  • Best jobs for you - green score (70+) = good match, orange = maybe, red = weak.
  • Make today's plan - a short to-do list for today.

🔎 Applying for a job

  1. Open a job and click Create my CV + cover letter.
  2. Read and correct them. Replace any [ADD: ...] with true facts.
  3. Download the Word files, or click Send application now (needs Gmail in Settings).
  4. Click I have applied. You get a follow-up reminder after 7 days.

💬 AI questions

When the AI needs true information (for example a driver licence or a certificate), it asks you. Answer on the AI questions page.

🎤 Interview practice

On a job page, click Interview practice for likely questions, sample answers from your real background and YouTube practice links.

⚙️ Settings

  • Claude key (optional): turns on smart AI. Without it, free basic mode is used.
  • Gmail (optional): send applications from your own Gmail with an App Password.
  • WhatsApp: green buttons on job pages - no setup.

Step-by-step links are on the Settings page.

🔒 Password

Forgot it? Click Forgot password on the log-in page to get an email link, or ask the admin to set a new one.

📱 Opening it on a phone or another laptop at home

Use the "from other devices at home" link shown on your dashboard (it looks like http://192.168.x.x:8010/u/yourname/). The laptop running the app must be on, and both devices must use the same Wi-Fi.

If it does not open: on the laptop, allow Docker Desktop in Windows Firewall (Private networks).

Good practice

  • Keep every CV honest - never invent experience.
  • Do not put passport, visa or IRD numbers or your date of birth on a CV.
  • Run backup.bat once a week.
