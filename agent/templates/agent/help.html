Quick start (5 steps)

  1. Profile & CVs: open Profile & CVs. Check your name, phone, availability and job types. Add one or more CVs (upload PDF/Word or paste the text).
  2. Find jobs: open Jobs. Click Search jobs (type a job type or leave it empty), add a job from a website link, or paste a job description.
  3. Check the score: each job gets a match score (green 70+, orange 45–69, red below 45) with reasons and a tip.
  4. Create documents: open a job and click Create CV + cover letter. The AI uses your most relevant CV. Read everything, replace any [ADD: ...] notes with true facts, then download the Word files.
  5. Apply and record: apply on the employer's website, then click I have applied. A follow-up reminder is set for 7 days later.

Dashboard

  • Jobs found today, AI suggestions, applications to review, applied today/total, interviews and follow-ups due.
  • Ask AI for today's summary gives a short plan for the day.
  • The automatic search runs every day at 8:00 AM (Auckland time) for every active user.

AI questions

The AI never guesses facts about you. When it needs true information (for example, a licence or a certificate), it asks: "Question for <your name>: ...". Answer it on the AI questions page. Your answer is used in your future CVs only.

Interview preparation

On a job page, click Interview preparation for likely questions, sample answers based on your real background, questions to ask the employer, and YouTube practice searches. You can download it as a Word file.

Auto mode (optional)

Off by default. Only you can turn it on in Profile & CVs (tick the consent box). It prepares a CV and cover letter automatically for strong matches. You still review and submit every application yourself.

Job websites

The agent reads public pages on SEEK, Student Job Search and Trade Me Jobs and respects each site's rules. If a site blocks automated reading (for example LinkedIn or Indeed), open the job yourself and use Add a job from a link or Paste a job description.

Admin tasks

  • Give access: Give access → enter name, login name, password and job types. Each person's data is private and never mixed.
  • Turn a login off/on: on Admin overview, click Disable login / Enable login.
  • Audit log: see who did what and when. Opening another person's records is logged.
  • Import many CVs at once (Command Prompt in the project folder):
    docker compose exec web python manage.py setup_candidate --username NAME --full-name "Full Name" --cv-folder /app/data/import/FOLDER
    Copy the CV folder into data\import\ first.

Good practice

  • Never invent experience. Keep every CV honest.
  • Do not put passport, visa or IRD numbers or your date of birth on a CV.
  • Run backup.bat once a week.
